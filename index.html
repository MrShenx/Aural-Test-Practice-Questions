A listen-and-echo trainer covering every part of the AMEB aural test, from Preliminary through Grade 8 — pick a grade below and only the tasks that grade actually asks for will show up. Each question is generated in the exam's own style for that grade. Listen the way the examiner plays it, respond out loud, then check the reveal. Rhythm and Melody build working memory from a 2-bar phrase up to a 4–5 bar passage; Pitch, Triad, Cadence, Time and Memory cover the rest of the official test requirements as they're introduced grade by grade.

Grade 3 aural test — Rhythm: tap or clap back a duple- or triple-time passage (two hearings) and state whether it is duple or triple. Melody: hum or sing back a short phrase, played twice, combining melody and time. Pitch: hum or sing the higher or lower of any two notes within an octave from middle C, sounded together; and, after the examiner sounds a keynote, hum or sing then name the interval (2nd, 3rd, 4th or 5th of the major scale) sounded in succession to it.
